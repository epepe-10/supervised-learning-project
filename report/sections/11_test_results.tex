\section{Final evaluation on the untouched test set}
\label{sec:test}

The three tuned models were evaluated once on the 9{,}758 people of the test
set, each with the threshold frozen during training
(Section~\ref{sec:tuning}). Nothing was changed after this evaluation.

\paragraph{Test against cross-validation.} The test scores
(Table~\ref{tab:test}) are slightly below the out-of-fold scores of
Table~\ref{tab:models-tuned}: about $-0.01$ AP and $-0.01$ F1 for every model
(LightGBM: AP 0.832 to 0.821, F1 0.733 to 0.722). This small drop is the one
expected from selecting hyperparameters and thresholds on the same folds
(Section~\ref{sec:tuning}), and the ranking is unchanged. LightGBM reaches
86.3\% accuracy (76.1\% for the trivial model) and finds 74\% of the \pos{}
people with a precision of 70\% (confusion matrices and curves in
Figures~\ref{fig:test-confusion} and~\ref{fig:test-roc-pr}, appendix).
