\section{Experiment tracking}
\label{sec:tracking}

Every tuning trial and every final training is logged with MLflow, in a local
database (\code{mlflow.db}, with the files in \code{mlruns/}), under a single
experiment, \code{adult-income}. The runs are organised as follows:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{one parent run per tuning study} (e.g.\ ``lightgbm - tuning''):
  the best hyperparameters, the best mean CV AP, and the files written by the
  tuning as artifacts (best parameters, table of all trials, optimisation
  history);
  \item \textbf{one child run per Optuna trial} (e.g.\ ``lightgbm - trial
  12''): the hyperparameters of the trial, the AP of each of the 5 folds, their
  mean and their standard deviation. Logging the five folds, and not only the
  mean, shows how much a score depends on the split;
  \item \textbf{one run per final training}: the tuned hyperparameters, the
  out-of-fold metrics, the chosen threshold and the saved model file as an
  artifact.
\end{itemize}
In total, the 95 tuning trials (25 + 20 + 50), the 3 tuning studies and the 3
final trainings are logged. Since the sampler and the folds are seeded,
re-running the tuning with tracking switched on proposes the same trials
